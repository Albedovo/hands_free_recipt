Interaction design prototype

Hands-Free Recipe

A recipe screen you control with your feet. It turns to face you as you move around the kitchen.

The idea

Cooking with a phone means touching the screen with dirty hands, and the screen faces the wrong way as you move. Here, a foot pedal sits at each work station. Step on it and the screen turns to you; step again to turn the page or hear the step read aloud.

Pedal 1 · 30°SinkWashing and rinsing
Pedal 2 · 90°CounterChopping and prep
Pedal 3 · 150°StoveCooking and stirring

System

flowchart LR
  P1["Sink pedal"] --> MCU
  P2["Counter pedal"] --> MCU
  P3["Stove pedal"] --> MCU
  MCU["Arduino Uno"] -- "angle" --> S["Servo
turns the screen"]
  MCU -- "USB" --> W["Recipe web page"]
  W --> D["Screen"]
  W --> V["Voice"]

Pedal logic

flowchart TD
  A(["Step on a pedal"]) --> F{"Screen facing
this pedal?"}
  F -- "No" --> T["Turn screen
to this station"]
  F -- "Yes" --> G{"Press type"}
  G -- "Tap" --> N["Next step"]
  G -- "Double tap" --> B["Previous step"]
  G -- "Hold" --> RD["Read step aloud"]

The Uno remembers which station the screen was last turned to. While the voice is reading, a tap just stops it.
